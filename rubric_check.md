## Final fact and citation check

- **Assignment rules:** the two attached guidelines were read. The Plan guideline specifies 4–6 pages, the six suggested content areas, approximately 5–10 sources, and the six rubric weights above. The Group Project guideline identifies simulation/modelling as a suitable type and does not require a finished application.
- **HKU rules:** first-come-first-served during the August selection period, visible rank/vacancies, several approval runs each day, waiting-list purge, loss of queue place after dropping, and auto-ballot during add/drop are supported by the two official HKU pages cited in Sections 1 and 3. The CCTM priority-period exception is stated and excluded. The one-course decision is labelled a simplification.
- **Academic references:** all six journal articles in the proposal were located by title, authors, venue, year, and DOI in publisher pages, author/institution records, or article PDFs. The two HKU references are official webpages with no DOI. All eight reference-list entries are cited in the text; no uncited item remains.
- **Claims of effects:** the proposal does not claim observed irrationality, actual repeated switching, or SIS overload at HKU. Results from school choice and hospital queues are presented as related evidence or methodological motivation, not direct evidence about HKU.
- **Future verification:** the reported model outcomes will need to be generated and checked during the project. The draft contains no invented HKU data, simulated result, or quotation.
